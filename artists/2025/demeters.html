Demeters Döttrar
About Past

Listening to Demeters Döttrar is like stepping into a parallel universe; a tiny unexplored corner with paper-thin walls, or a delicate bubble about to burst at any moment. The band, based in Copenhagen and Stockholm, consists of Ida Skibsted and Charlott Malmenholt. They explore the intimate space of the voice, where strange linguistic realities are continuously built up and taken apart to become something new.

On their debut, released on Discreet Music, objects and archaeological finds whisper with great intimacy, emotions put on strange shoes with pointed heels, and memories of girlfriends, men, bridges, plum-red boards in station buildings, and Sister Venus over the evening city are sorted out with silver forks and found through loops, anagrams, field recordings, and cassette tapes into dreamy sound collages.
