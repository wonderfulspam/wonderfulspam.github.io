CCsquele(DK)

With CCsquele, everything feels like a curious probing of musical styles, carried by the power of a mining truck and the elegance of velvet curtains being drawn back. CCsquele erratically and effortlessly connects the dots between monumental glam rock, dungeon synth, black metal and eurodance, always one step ahead, tumbling in free fall while somehow landing on their feet.

Also the mastermind and sole emperor behind the cassette label Dybdestegte, CCsquele has carried this restless approach through a dizzying number of solo projects and bands over the last decade, including Værket, Reverie, Slægt, Thicket, Scimitar, Jesum Christum, Shrug and Cuirass.

Felisha Ledesma(US)

Felisha Ledesma is a sound artist and musician based in Malmö, Sweden, whose work often utilizes field recordings, synthesized sound and Ledesma's own voice enveloped in washes of distortion and cavernous space.

A distinctly warm and organic palette permeates Ledesma’s work, with each moment slowly unfolding into an intimate aural experience that paints abstract yet vivid pictures. Everyday sounds and eroded memories are distilled into sonic recollections, often unfolding in a non-linear way that invites meditative deep listening.

GUSH(UK/AR)

FRIDAY

GUSH is a stripped down avant pop duo with maximal execution featuring Steven Warwick (fka Heatsick) and Iván Brito (aka Vanya). The project brings to mind psychedelic experimentalists such as Silver Apples with post punk incisive humour and the studio precision of Flood. Creating surreal lyrical observations informed by mythology and the imagination, GUSH mix short song forms with a looser, psychedelic musical approach.

Iván Brito is a drummer and producer hailing from Argentina, while Steven Warwick is a British artist, writer and musician whose practice incorporates sculptural installation, performance, video and composition. Their debut album as GUSH, Splash of Milk, was recently released by Huntleys + Palmers.

Laila Sakini(AU)

SATURDAY
20:45

Laila Sakini is a Melbourne-born, London-based multi-instrumentalist, composer, vocalist, producer and performer. Working with piano, voice, guitar, found sound and silence, Laila is a world builder, creating dynamic and textured environments that provide the listener with space to develop their own meanings and responses. Performing live she draws out delicate parts from her discography inviting audiences into an intimate arena where musical fragments are exhibited with improvised, real-time emotion.

Laila Sakini has released numerous albums, including Vivienne, Strada, Princess Diana of Wales, Paloma and the collaborative records Cupo (with Valentina Magaletti) and Figures (with Lucy Van). She has performed worldwide at Cafe Oto, Galeria Zé dos Bois, Berlin Atonal, Listen! festival, The White Hotel, De Nor Antwerp, Meakusma x Arkaoda, Kings Place, Ponto d’Orvalho among many other art spaces, festivals and venues.

Maria Bertel(DK)

FRIDAY
01:00

Maria Bertel is a danish trombone player and composer who’s been doing the rounds in the Copenhagen music scene since the late 2000s, where she co-founded the venue Mayhem and the artist collective Eget Værelse.

Bertel combines her acoustic instrument and tonal range with amps and varying degrees of distortion, creating an output that draws inspiration from drone and noise music. The raw amplification makes audible what normally goes unheard, and physical movements, metal and breath become a swirling part of her compositions. Changing organic patterns emerge, with tiny sounds appearing brutally clear as well as the range of overtones of the instrument.

Apart from her work as a solo artist, she performs and composes in groups like the notorious four piece noise rock band Selvhenter and improvised noise duo Nina Garcia & Maria Bertel. She has also collaborated with acts such as Tashi Dorji, Jooklo Duo, Nick Klein & Valentina Magaletti.

Megabasse(FR)

SATURDAY
19:30

Behind the moniker Megabasse stands French musician Pierre Bujeau. Also a member of groups Omertá and Tanz Mein Herz, Bujeau uses Megabasse as a long-form minimalist exploration of resonances and harmonics creating an almost mystical, communal and uncanny space where time appears to break away from reality.

His approach is deceptively simple, as Bujeau picks up the double-necked guitar, an instrument that normally signals maximum rockist excess along the likes of Jimmy Page. In his hands, however, it transforms into something closer to a zither or a dulcimer, producing soft chiming patterns that build against themselves until the sound of the room, passed back and forth between his two amps, starts to blur everything.

NZIRIA is an experimental project by artist and dj Tullia Benedicta, born and raised in Ravenna from a Neapolitan family. NZIRIA works in the self-described genre of “hard neomelodic”, a hybridisation of the Neapoletan neomelodic song and hardcore gabber influences.

Through hard neomelodic, NZIRIA creates a sound that moves between different concepts: North and South, traditional and contemporary music, masculine and feminine identity.

Tullia Benedicta debuted on Gabber Eleganza’s imprint Never Sleep with the full-length XXYBRID and recently released the album SYYSMA on DRACO Records.

Peder Mannerfelt splits the difference between playful immediacy and daring experimentation. You're as likely to find him premiering a live show at Berlinʼs Atonal festival as pulling apart fabled techno meccas from Berghain to Fabric with synapse-frying precision.

As a producer, heʼs worked on every one of Fever Rayʼs acclaimed albums and live shows, as well as projects from alt music heavyweights such as Smerz, Blonde Redhead and Glasser. Mannerfelt thrives on the energy and community of contemporary club music, and more recently, his PM+ series celebrates the immediacy of club-focused tracks, as he pushes the musicʼs possibilities in wild, unpredictable directions.

Radical Democracy(US)

THURSDAY
22:00

Radical Democracy, the duo of multidisciplinary artists James Hoff and Ben Kudler, creates live audiovisual works that utilize publicly available video cameras positioned throughout the world. Drawing on this data from publicly accessible live surveillance video streams, the project turns live music performance into a journey through global networks of observations and daily life using compromised technological platforms and protocols as creative material. Their work proposes infiltration as a new form of democratic participation within the networked infrastructures that shape political and cultural bodies. Shelter Press will release Radical Democracy’s debut record in Fall 2026.

Ben Kudler is an artist, computer scientist and humanities professor in New York. His work primarily deals with sound, from improvised electronic music to audio installations to speech and text synthesis. He has a background in the noise scene and also studied computer science. He teaches, researches and performs at, among others, the New School for Social Research, NYU, UCLA and Goldsmiths College in London.

James Hoff is an artist based in New York, who examines how technology, media and data shape our social and political world, while developing new ways to use these tools creatively. For his work, Hoff uses, among other things, computer viruses, data signals, catchy tunes, dead spots and hacked Google Maps as starting points for visual, audio and multimedia-based projects. He is also a co-founder of the independent publishing organization Primary Information. His works have been exhibited and performed internationally, including at Artists Space, Bergen Kunsthall, the Contemporary Arts Center in New Orleans, the Center d'Art Contemporain in Geneva, The Kitchen, Kunsthall Oslo, the Royal Theater La Monnaie, Mass MOCA and MoMA PS1.

Ragnhild May(DK)

SATURDAY
16:30

Ragnhild May is a Danish multidisciplinary artist and composer with a background in visual arts. Her artistic practice revolves around the material conditions that allow sound to circulate and draws upon sculpture, installation, and performance to inquire into the cultural and social history of musical instruments.

She often reflects on the form of musical instruments, specifically how they have been designed for what she describes as normative bodies, as well as on their sonic limitations. To explore this, she experiments with scale and multiplicity, sometimes inventing her own versions of these familiar objects, which can be both played and exhibited independently.

May has performed at festivals such as Klang (DK), Spor (DK), Organ Sound Art Festival (DK), Archipel (CH), and Gaudeamus (NL), as well as at experimental music spaces including Issue Project Room (US), Roulette Intermedium (US), and Superdeluxe (JP). She has also performed and exhibited at many institutions including Tabakalera (ES), SMK - The National Gallery of Denmark (DK), Overgaden Institute of Contemporary Art (DK), and Rønnebæksholm (DK).

Victoria Keddie(US)

SATURDAY
17:15

Victoria Keddie is an interdisciplinary artist working across sound, video, installation, performance, and research. In her work she examines the architectures of communication—acoustic, spatial, and technological—exploring how gesture, noise, and interference shape human experience.

For over a decade, Keddie co-directed E.S.P. TV, an international platform for hybrid media and broadcast-based performance, and she currently teaches at the University of Pennsylvania, where she leads the course Listening Beyond Hearing and a spatial sound studio she designed for the program.

She has performed and exhibited internationally at venues including Fridman Gallery, Pioneer Works, and The Whitney Museum of American Art (US); The Barbican (UK); Berghain Kantine (DE); The Goethe-Institut (CO); and MAPS (DK). In 2025, she was Composer-in-Residence at EMS (Elektronmusikstudion) in Stockholm.

Catàleg de Fulles - Tea Workshop

Catàleg de Fulles (Leaf Catalog in Catalan) is a platform for the dissemination of tea culture which also works as a tea sourcing endeavour. It has been developed by Arnau Sala Saez, who will also perform as Ex Continent Friday night. The project was born out of years of personal experience with visual arts and music, as well as exploring tea culture—both as an amateur as well as professionally. It seeks to find connections between these practices, understanding tea as a central element for collective learning, experimentation, and the exchange of knowledge.

During these workshops we will gather and experience tea together, exploring different types. The intention is to demystify the preparation of tea, sharing tools, envisioning possibilities, understanding the characteristics of the various types of tea, and initiating conversations around tea and any other topics which may arise.

The workshops do not follow a rigid format. Instead, we share tea, talk about what we know—and also what we don’t—and let conversations flow naturally. Catàleg de Fulles does not seek to attract a specific audience: tea is for anyone who wants to approach it, without prior knowledge or elitist interests.

Some tea will also be available for purchase during the festival.

We offer three sessions:

Thursday: 17.30-19.00

Friday: 17.00-18.30

Saturday: 14.30-16.00

You can reserve a spot for 120 DKK + fee.

PLEASE NOTE: To keep the sessions intimate and focused, only six spots will be available for each session. It is therefore a good idea to buy your ticket sooner rather than later.

Community Dinner by Manna Café

Join us for a delicious meal with good company at the FOEG community dinner!

This year the menu for the community dinner is provided by Manna Café & Bar.

Manna Café and Bar is a local, family-run spot built around a love of good food and good company, with great care for each other and the people around them. Everything is made from scratch using the best seasonal ingredients, and every day they bake their own sourdough bread, which is at the heart of many of their dishes—from brunch and open sandwiches to small plates served with cold drinks from the bar.

Community is a big part of what Manna does. Their weekly communal dinners bring neighbours, friends and strangers together around long tables to share food and spend time together at their cosy spot in Amager, Copenhagen.

The menu: Homemade sourdough bread, oven-roasted cauliflower with herb dressing, potatoes with chilli aioli and beetroot salad with pears, walnuts and mustard dressing.

The community dinner will be held Saturday evening September 26. Tickets are 130 DKK + fee (presale), and we recommend you secure your ticket online, in case they sell out.
