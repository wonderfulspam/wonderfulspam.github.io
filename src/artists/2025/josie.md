Josie is Copenhagen’s newest head-in-the-clouds, in-the-gut heartbreak jangle pop group. Formed in 2023, the band consists of four friends - Charlotte, Dawn, Martin, and Anton. Their sound has solid roots in indie pop but updates the classic formula ever so slightly with a current punk sensibility, making sure to keep a bit of fuck you in the mix. Josie is set to deliver their punch of melodic ramshackle jangle with an air of wistful and melancholic energy.
