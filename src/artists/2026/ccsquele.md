With CCsquele, everything feels like a curious probing of musical styles, carried by the power of a mining truck and the elegance of velvet curtains being drawn back. CCsquele erratically and effortlessly connects the dots between monumental glam rock, dungeon synth, black metal and eurodance, always one step ahead, tumbling in free fall while somehow landing on their feet. 

Also the mastermind and sole emperor behind the cassette label Dybdestegte, CCsquele has carried this restless approach through a dizzying number of solo projects and bands over the last decade, including Værket, Reverie, Slægt, Thicket, Scimitar, Jesum Christum, Shrug and Cuirass.
