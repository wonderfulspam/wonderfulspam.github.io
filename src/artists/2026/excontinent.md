Ex Continent is the moniker of musician and visual artist Arnau Sala Saez. For FOEG, he will present a version of the performance SDSDS, an exercise in the organization of sounds and events. Using synthesis and several devices, the piece encourages the listener to engage phenomenologically with non-hierarchical horizons of perception opening up around them. 

Ex Continent’s work explores specific sensory environments that dramatise the relationships between their individual components. By experimenting with sound from non-musical disciplines, this practice examines the dynamics, expectations and interactions that usually appear around the act of listening, as well as physical and metaphysical perspectives.
