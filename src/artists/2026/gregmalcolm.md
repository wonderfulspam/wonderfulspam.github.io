Hailing from New Zealand, the music of Greg Malcolm sits somewhere between blues, folk and minimalist repetitive music, combining a Cage-inspired approach to the prepared guitar with a sonic world that evokes both the vastness of the desert and the jolting rhythm of an unpaved track.

Malcolm is a one-man orchestra, simultaneously playing three guitars with his hands and feet. Surrounded by bells, springs, resonators, gongs and knives, he draws on a rich palette of timbres and textures, building hypnotic solo sets around repetition, evolving melodies and layered drones.

As a guitarist, composer and improviser, Malcolm has been an active figure in New Zealand’s experimental music scene for decades, with numerous solo releases and collaborations with artists including Tetuzi Akiyama, Alan Licht, Eugene Chadbourne, Bruce Russell and many others. He is also one half of Slightly Imperfect, alongside Jenny Ward, performing the songs of Scottish poet and songwriter Ivor Cutler.
