Duncan Moore is an intermedia artist and musician from Baltimore, Maryland. He works primarily in the realm of abstract sound and movement and usually performs under the moniker Headband. His work moves between manipulated sound, physical movement and performance, often bringing together tape, electronics and unconventional sound sources.

Moore also plays traditional and free-form music on the Highland bagpipes, drawing on the classical techniques of Piobaireachd, the original music of the Highland pipes dating back to the 17th century.
