NZIRIA is an experimental project by artist and dj Tullia Benedicta, born and raised in Ravenna from a Neapolitan family. NZIRIA works in the self-described genre of “hard neomelodic”, a hybridisation of the Neapoletan neomelodic song and hardcore gabber influences. Through hard neomelodic, NZIRIA creates a sound that moves between different concepts: North and South, traditional and contemporary music, masculine and feminine identity.
