Radical Democracy, the duo of multidisciplinary artists James Hoff and Ben Kudler, creates live audiovisual works that utilize publicly available video cameras positioned throughout the world.  Drawing on this data from publicly accessible live surveillance video streams, the project turns live music performance into a journey through global networks of observations and daily life using compromised technological platforms and protocols as creative material. Their work proposes infiltration as a new form of democratic participation within the networked infrastructures that shape political and cultural bodies. Shelter Press will release Radical Democracy’s debut record in Fall 2026. 

Ben Kudler is an artist, computer scientist and humanities professor in New York. His work primarily deals with sound, from improvised electronic music to audio installations to speech and text synthesis. He has a background in the noise scene and also studied computer science. He teaches, researches and performs at, among others, the New School for Social Research, NYU, UCLA and Goldsmiths College in London.

James Hoff is an artist based in New York, who examines how technology, media and data shape our social and political world, while developing new ways to use these tools creatively. For his work, Hoff uses, among other things, computer viruses, data signals, catchy tunes, dead spots and hacked Google Maps as starting points for visual, audio and multimedia-based projects. He is also a co-founder of the independent publishing organization Primary Information. His works have been exhibited and performed internationally, including at Artists Space, Bergen Kunsthall, the Contemporary Arts Center in New Orleans, the Center d'Art Contemporain in Geneva, The Kitchen, Kunsthall Oslo, the Royal Theater La Monnaie, Mass MOCA and MoMA PS1.
