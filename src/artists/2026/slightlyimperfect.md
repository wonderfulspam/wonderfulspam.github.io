Slightly Imperfect is the New Zealand duo of Greg Malcolm and Jenny Ward, on a sojourn into the strange, playful and surreal world of Scottish poet, songwriter and performer Ivor Cutler.

Cutler’s distinctive blend of whimsical poetry, simple melodies, absurd humour and everyday observations is brought to life through Ward’s vocals and Malcolm’s one-man-orchestra accompaniments. Using guitars, domestic objects, toys, bells, pipes and extended techniques, Slightly Imperfect opens the door to a timeless realm as an ode to play and absurdity, and a tribute to one of the 20th century’s most distinctive poets, songwriters and performers, whose strange and tender vision still resonates today.
